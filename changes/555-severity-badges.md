Changed

- **Problem badges show how serious** ([#555](https://github.com/uncoverthestack/databricks-bundle-inspector/issues/555)). The count on a task box, an overview box and a search result now has the icon and colour of its worst problem: a circled X for an error, a triangle for warnings, as in the Problems panel. Before, it was a plain number on a faint tint that was hard to see in light themes. Point at it for the counts in words, such as *1 error, 2 warnings*.
