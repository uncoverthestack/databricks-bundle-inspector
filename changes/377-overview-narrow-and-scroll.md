Changed

- **The bundle overview keeps its place, and fits a narrow or short panel** ([#377](https://github.com/uncoverthestack/databricks-bundle-inspector/issues/377)). Coming back from a job or pipeline, the overview is scrolled where you left it, with the groups you opened still open. In a narrow panel it uses less margin and draws no lines, and the header's search box shows only its icon instead of a cut-off hint. In a short panel, a resource's details take at most part of it and scroll inside.
