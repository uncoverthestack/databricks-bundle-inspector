- **A job's graph opens with every task in view** ([#552](https://github.com/uncoverthestack/databricks-bundle-inspector/issues/552)). It used to open at full size from its top-left corner, so on a normal panel the last tasks started out of view, with any warning on them. It now opens fitted to the panel, never zoomed in past full size or out so far that names can't be read, and fits again when the task details close. Once you drag or zoom, it stays where you put it.
