Changed

- **Issues is dimmed when there is nothing to filter** ([#390](https://github.com/uncoverthestack/databricks-bundle-inspector/issues/390)). On a bundle or job with no problems, **Issues** can't be pressed, and pointing at it says "No problems found". Opening the graph from the status bar on such a bundle shows **All**, instead of an empty bundle overview.
- **Pressing Issues again closes its list** ([#390](https://github.com/uncoverthestack/databricks-bundle-inspector/issues/390)), as the x on the list does. The view stays on Issues, and another press opens the list again. Before, a second press kept the list open.
